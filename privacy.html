Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 25, 2026

ClutterRack LLC (“Clutter Rack,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you visit our website, contact us, request a quote, or inquire about our garage storage products and professional installation services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact information: name, email address, phone number, and other information you provide.
  • Quote and service information: garage details, storage needs, installation location, preferred products, and related requests.
  • Communications: messages sent through website forms, chat, email, phone, or other channels.
  • Website usage information: IP address, browser, device type, pages visited, and interactions, where collected through website technologies.
  • Marketing attribution data: Google Click ID (gclid), Facebook Click ID (fbclid), UTM parameters, and similar tracking identifiers from advertising campaigns.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and quote requests.
  • Recommend suitable garage storage solutions and coordinate professional installations.
  • Communicate about requested products or services.
  • Send marketing calls and text messages when you have provided prior express written consent under the TCPA.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our website and related services.
  • Protect against fraud, misuse, or security issues.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. TCPA / SMS and Telephone Communications

If you check the consent box on our forms or otherwise provide prior express written consent, Clutter Rack may contact you by telephone call or text message (SMS) at the mobile number you provide, including using an automatic telephone dialing system (ATDS) or artificial/prerecorded voice, for marketing and promotional purposes related to our garage storage products and services.

Consent is not a condition of purchase or of receiving a quote. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You may opt out of marketing calls by contacting us or following any instructions provided on a call.

We retain records of your consent (including timestamp, IP address, and the exact disclosure language) as required for TCPA compliance.

4. Website Forms and Chat

Information submitted through website forms or chat is used to respond to your request and coordinate next steps.

Please avoid submitting sensitive financial information, passwords, or other highly confidential information through website forms or chat.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies to support functionality, remember preferences, understand activity, and provide features. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

6. Sharing Information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate our business (hosting, communication platforms, analytics). We may disclose information when required by law, to protect our rights, or in connection with a business transaction. We do not sell your personal information.

7. Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable measures to protect personal information. No system is completely secure. We retain information as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations (including TCPA consent records).

8. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time. Disabling cookies may affect some features.

9. Children’s Privacy

Our products and services are not directed toward children under 13. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last Updated” date will reflect changes.
